# Features overview

This is the integrated version of the customer and admin interface, not a stack of update ZIP files.

## Customers

1. Public home page, login, and registration.
2. Catalog containing 54 **demo** products with local image assets.
3. Server-side search and filters by category, price and availability.
4. Instant search of the loaded catalog, medicine quick-view modal and recently viewed history.
5. Browser-local favorites and comparisons of up to three listed products; these do not imply medical equivalence.
6. Multi-select Add Selected to Cart with a floating action bar and estimated subtotal.
7. Shopping cart quantity controls, checkout, order status and cancellation.
8. Prescription-document requests and approval checks for designated medicines.
9. Customer report/support ticket history and messaging.

## Admins

1. Role-restricted admin dashboard and navigation.
2. Create, view, edit and delete medicines, search and CSV export.
3. Low-stock and out-of-stock filtering, selecting multiple items to restock toward 100 units.
4. Inventory Insights, retail-value estimate, reorder suggestions and CSV export.
5. Order queue, status filters, fulfillment, COD payment confirmation.
6. Prescription request review, account management, and support inbox.
7. Image preview, image-path warnings and unsaved-changes protection on medicine forms.
8. Automatic dashboard refresh for supported admin panels.

## Payments

Pay at Pickup and Cash on Delivery are implemented as offline payment choices. Optional Xendit hosted checkout requires configuration, testing and valid credentials for the relevant channels. No keys are in this repository.

## Product photos

There are normal sample illustrations and synthetic product-photo-style images. They do **not** depict guaranteed matching commercial packaging, and must not be presented as actual manufacturer imagery or used as medical-labeling references. You can upload licensed real brand-specific product photos, then set the product's image URL through admin editing.
